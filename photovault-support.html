Byroll Support

Contact support

Email yan.chen.im@gmail.com with a description of the issue. Your Byroll build number is in Settings. Your iOS version and any error message can help. Keep your PIN and private media out of the message.

Waiting for iCloud

Open Settings → Sync to see which items are on this iPhone, which are waiting to transfer, and whether the vault key is available here. Check your internet connection and available iPhone and iCloud storage. Use the same Apple Account on both devices and check iCloud Drive and iCloud Keychain separately.

Moving to a new iPhone

On the old iPhone, wait for file sync to finish. On the new iPhone, sign in to the same Apple Account, then open Byroll with your existing PIN after its key arrives. Confirm your items have arrived and open a few photos and videos before erasing the old device.

Missing key or forgotten PIN

If Byroll is waiting for a key, check iCloud Keychain on both devices and keep the old iPhone available. Support cannot reset the main vault PIN or recreate a lost key. If you still have access to a decoy’s parent vault, you can change that decoy’s PIN in Settings.

Moving photos and videos

Byroll uses Full Access to Photos to check a move before removing the source. Review this permission in iPhone Settings. If the app can’t verify a move, check both libraries before retrying. After a successful move into the vault, the original may still appear in Apple Photos’ Recently Deleted collection.
