PhotoVault Privacy Policy

PhotoVault is provided by Yan Chen. The app does not require a PhotoVault account or display ads. The developer does not receive your vault contents or PIN through the app.

Optional usage analytics

While usage analytics is enabled, PhotoVault sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase to help improve the app. Google Analytics records basic app activity such as sessions, plus an app installation identifier, app and device information, and approximate region. PhotoVault also records when setup finishes, when you change the app language, and when you open help. Analytics never contains photos, videos, filenames, PINs, vault identifiers, or information about decoy vaults.

Share Usage Analytics is on by default. You can turn it on or off at any time in Privacy & Support. Turning it off stops collection, clears analytics data still on your device, and resets the analytics identifier. Information already sent follows the Google Analytics retention settings. You can email us using the address below with privacy or deletion requests.

Photos, videos, and permissions

With your permission, PhotoVault reads your Apple Photos library so you can browse and select items. When you move an item, the app uses the photo or video and the metadata needed to preserve it, such as its date, location, favorites status, and album membership. Full Photos access lets PhotoVault check the destination and request removal of the original. You can change or withdraw Photos access in iPhone Settings.

If you turn on Lock When Face Down, motion readings are used on your iPhone to detect when to lock the vault.

Where your files and key are stored

Vault media and metadata are encrypted on your iPhone. When iCloud Drive is available for your vault, PhotoVault syncs encrypted copies through your Apple Account. The PIN-protected vault key is stored separately in Apple Keychain and can sync through iCloud Keychain. You can manage access to these services in your Apple Account settings.

PhotoVault support cannot retrieve a forgotten main vault PIN or reconstruct a missing vault key. If every copy of the key is lost, the correct PIN alone cannot open the encrypted files. A vault that created a decoy can change that decoy’s PIN or delete it.

Maps and location

If a photo or video has location metadata, opening its Info view can request a map of the approximate area from Apple Maps. PhotoVault uses the exact coordinate on the iPhone to place the pin. Tapping the map passes the exact location to Apple Maps. Apple Maps privacy information

Retention and deletion

You can move vault items back to Apple Photos or delete them in PhotoVault. Vault deletions are permanent in the app. Synced deletion changes may take time to reach iCloud and your other devices. When you move an item into the vault and approve deleting its original, Apple Photos may keep that original in its Recently Deleted collection for up to 30 days. You can review or remove it there.
