Get My UDID
  1. Tap Get My UDID and allow the configuration profile to download.
  2. Open Settings → tap Profile Downloaded at the top.
  3. Tap Install — you'll be sent back here with your UDID.

Private by design: the profile only asks iOS for your device identifiers and hands them straight back to this page. The UDID is returned in the address fragment (after the #), which browsers never send to a server, and nothing is stored.
